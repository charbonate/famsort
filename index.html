Settings

Invite a co-parent

Viewer can see and download the photos. Editor can add, change and delete them. They'll get an email with a secure link — they sign in with their own cloud account.

Who has access

OneDrive supports two levels: Viewer and Editor. “Add-only” and “admin” aren’t possible on a personal cloud account. When you let a co-parent invite others, you still approve each request — the actual access is always granted from your account.

Albums shared with you

Albums a co-parent has shared with you show up here. If they've made you a manager, you can request to add other viewers or editors — the album's owner approves each request from their device.

Use Scan this album for faces on any shared album to recognise who's in its photos and sort them into your own albums. Photos are downloaded only to detect faces and removed from your device afterwards — unless your output location is this device. Requesting to add someone else needs edit access to the shared album.

How photos are sorted

Choose how a photo with more than one tagged person is filed. Your albums always mirror your output folders.

Share recognition with your partner

Export a tiny file that teaches your partner's phone to recognise your family the same way — no photos included, just the face "fingerprints". They import it and their photos sort automatically too.

Automatic backup & sorting

On by default — set up once and forget. Like your phone's auto-upload, Famsort keeps watching your folders, sorts new photos on this device, and saves organised copies to your chosen output — OneDrive (backed up) or a device folder (local only). Every sorted photo is listed on the Activity tab so you can verify it. Duplicates are skipped automatically. Turn it off only if you'd rather sort and back up by hand.

True background upload while the app is fully closed needs the native app — browsers limit background work. Keep the tab open (or installed) for continuous sync.

Your data

Everything lives only in this browser (thumbnails + face fingerprints). Originals stay in your gallery. You can wipe it all anytime.

“Reupdate & reorganise” rebuilds each person from the faces you’ve confirmed (tap a name in a photo to confirm it ✓), then re-checks every auto-tagged face — so a wrong face in an album gets cleaned out. No re-import needed.

“Re-scan faces” looks again for faces in the photos you’ve already added — use this if photos imported but no faces were found (for example if the accurate detector hadn’t loaded yet).

“Restore skipped faces” brings back everyone that was auto-hidden as ignored — use this if people have stopped being found after you pressed Ignore a lot.
