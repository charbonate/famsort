Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

The short version

FamSort sorts your photos into an album for each person. The face recognition that makes this work runs entirely on your own device. We do not run servers that receive, store, or process your photos, and your photos never pass through us.

Your original photos and your sorted albums live in your own cloud storage (such as your personal Microsoft OneDrive) and on your own devices — accounts that belong to you, not to us.

Who we are

FamSort (“FamSort”, “we”, “us”) provides the FamSort application. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the address in the Contact section below.

What FamSort processes

  • Your photos and videos — read from the folders or cloud accounts you point FamSort at, so it can detect faces and organise them.
  • Face data — mathematical descriptions of faces (and lightweight thumbnails) that FamSort generates on your device to group photos of the same person. This is created and used locally.
  • The names you assign — e.g. labelling a group as “Ava” — so albums are meaningful to you.
  • Basic account details — your name and profile picture from the cloud account you sign in with, used only to show who is signed in.
  • Your settings — such as which folders to watch and where to save sorted albums.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device — FamSort keeps a working copy of thumbnails, face data, names and settings in your browser’s local storage so the app is fast and works offline. You can clear this at any time by signing out or using “Delete all FamSort data on this device”.
  • In your own cloud — when you choose to save sorted albums, FamSort writes organised copies into a folder in your cloud account. We never receive a copy.
  • On our servers — nothing. We do not operate a backend that stores your photos or face data.

Face recognition

Face detection and grouping happen on your device, inside the app. The face data FamSort generates is used only to sort your own photos. It is not uploaded to us, not sold, and not shared. You can delete it at any time by clearing FamSort’s data on the device, which removes the stored face data and thumbnails.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use your photos or faces to train models for anyone else.
  • We do not run advertising trackers or profile you for ads.
  • We do not keep copies of your photos on our own infrastructure.

Third-party services

FamSort connects to the cloud account you sign in with (for example Microsoft OneDrive via the Microsoft Graph API) to read the photos you select and to save sorted albums back to your own storage. Your use of that account is governed by that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. The FamSort app itself is delivered as static files from a web host; that host may record standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) as part of normal web delivery.

Photos of children

FamSort is intended to be used by adults organising their own family photos, which may include photos of children. All processing of those photos happens on the device of the adult using the app. FamSort is not directed at children as users, and we do not knowingly collect data directly from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Access & portability — your photos and albums are already in your own cloud and device; you control them directly.
  • Deletion — use “Sign out” or “Delete all FamSort data on this device” to remove the local copy. You can also delete the albums FamSort created from your cloud account yourself.
  • Withdraw access — you can revoke FamSort’s access to your cloud account at any time from your account provider’s security settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the Australian Privacy Act or the GDPR. To exercise them, contact us below.

Security

Because your photos stay on your device and in your own cloud, their security depends largely on your device passcode and your cloud account’s security (such as a strong password and two-factor authentication). FamSort communicates with your cloud over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be made clear within the app or on this page.
